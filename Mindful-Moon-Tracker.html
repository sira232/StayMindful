ℹ️ About
Stay Mindful logs your mood alongside the Moon's sign, nakshatra and tithi (Lahiri sidereal). The guidance is general awareness based on classical Vedic ideas, not prediction or medical advice. Transit-tab planet positions use the MIT-licensed astronomy-engine library.

📍 Graha positions & next change
Sidereal (Lahiri), geocentric, nine grahas. Rahu/Ketu are the mean node (Drik Panchang's default). Lagna uses your location from Settings. Times are in your device's time zone. Planet positions agree with Swiss Ephemeris to within about 10 arcseconds and Lagna to within about 2; degrees are truncated, as in most panchangs. Own caveat: a planet sitting almost exactly on a boundary (or stationary there) can flip in the last few seconds.
🛡️ Gochara & Vedha · from your Moon sign
Vedha = a planet in a favorable house is blocked (its good result reduced) when another planet sits in its paired "obstruction" house.
Classical Gochara per BPHS (ch. 65-66): Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn counted from the natal Moon. Sun-Saturn and Moon-Mercury do not obstruct each other. Rahu/Ketu are not part of the classical table. Whole-sign houses; the wording of each line is a simple summary.
